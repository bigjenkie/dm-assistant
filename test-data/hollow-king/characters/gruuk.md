# Gruuk
**Player:** Sam
**Race/Class:** Half-orc Barbarian (Totem Warrior — Bear), Level 6
**Background:** Acolyte

**Backstory:** Gruuk was abandoned as an infant on the steps of a monastery of Pelor. The monks raised him, taught him to read, pray, and control his rage. He was the gentlest person in the monastery. When raiders attacked and burned it, Gruuk survived because his rage finally broke free — he killed three raiders with his bare hands. He doesn't know who the raiders were. He left to find answers but is terrified of what he became that day.

**Bonds:** "The monks of Pelor were my family. I will find who destroyed them."
**Flaws:** "I'm terrified of my own rage. When I lose control, people I love get hurt."
**Goals:** Find the raiders who burned the monastery. Reconcile his gentle nature with his rage.

**Key relationships:**
- Quiet around everyone but warms up to Sable (she doesn't judge)
- Looks up to Drogan (sees him as a spiritual mentor)
- Doesn't talk much but listens to everything

**Notable items:** Prayer beads (Pelor), Greataxe "Sunfall" (monastery relic), torn page from a monastery hymnal

**DM NOTE:** The raiders were hired by agents of the Hollow King. The monastery held a fragment of the binding ritual. Gruuk doesn't know this yet. Connecting this to the main plot is a major campaign moment waiting to happen.
