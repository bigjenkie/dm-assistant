# Drogan Ironvow
**Player:** Mike
**Race/Class:** Hill Dwarf Cleric (Life Domain), Level 6
**Background:** Acolyte

**Backstory:** Drogan was a respected priest of Moradin in the Khor-Dral mines. He was accused of stealing sacred relics and exiled — a crime he didn't commit. He suspects his former mentor, High Priest Thordak, framed him to cover up something bigger. Drogan left quietly rather than fight, but the shame eats at him.

**Bonds:** "I will prove my innocence and expose whoever corrupted the temple of Moradin."
**Flaws:** "I defer to authority even when I shouldn't. Old habits from the temple."
**Goals:** Clear his name. Return to Khor-Dral with honor.

**Key relationships:**
- Steady friendship with Vex (mutual respect)
- Mentors Gruuk (tries to fill the role the monks played)
- Philosophical debates with Sable about faith vs patron pacts

**Notable items:** Holy symbol of Moradin (handmade, not the stolen one), Shield +1, Warhammer "Oathkeeper"
