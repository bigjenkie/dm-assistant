# Vex Thornwood
**Player:** Sarah
**Race/Class:** Half-elf Ranger (Hunter), Level 6
**Background:** Outlander

**Backstory:** Vex grew up in a small village on the edge of the Ashenmere Valley. When she was 14, the dragon Scorrath attacked and destroyed her village. Her mother pushed her into a root cellar and didn't survive. Vex spent years tracking Scorrath but lost the trail. She carries a silver locket with a miniature portrait of her mother — her only possession from before.

**Bonds:** "I will find Scorrath and make him pay for what he took from me."
**Flaws:** "I have trouble trusting people who haven't proven themselves in the wild."
**Goals:** Find Scorrath. Protect people who can't protect themselves.

**Key relationships:**
- Closest to Drogan (respects his faith and steadiness)
- Suspicious of Sable (doesn't trust warlocks)
- Protective of Gruuk (sees her younger self in his quiet grief)

**Notable items:** Silver locket (mother's portrait), Longbow +1 "Ashfall"
