# Sable Nighthollow
**Player:** Jordan
**Race/Class:** Tiefling Warlock (Archfey), Level 6
**Background:** Charlatan

**Backstory:** Sable made a pact with an archfey named Whisper after a con went wrong and she was left for dead in the Feywild. Whisper saved her life but the terms of the pact are unclear — Sable hears Whisper's voice in dreams, giving cryptic instructions. Sometimes the instructions conflict with the party's goals. Sable hasn't told the party about the dreams.

**Bonds:** "Whisper saved my life. I owe her everything — but I don't know what she wants."
**Flaws:** "I lie by reflex. Even when the truth would serve me better."
**Goals:** Understand Whisper's true agenda. Find out if the pact can be broken without dying.

**Key relationships:**
- Closest to Gruuk (sees through his quiet exterior, they're both hiding something)
- Tension with Vex (Vex doesn't trust warlocks)
- Fascinated by Drogan's faith (envies his certainty)

**Notable items:** Rod of the Pact Keeper +1, Disguise kit, Deck of Illusions (3 cards remaining)

**DM SECRET:** Whisper is connected to the Hollow King — she was the fey who originally bound the lich 200 years ago. Her pact with Sable is part of a long game to ensure the binding holds. Sable will eventually have to choose between the party and Whisper's agenda.
