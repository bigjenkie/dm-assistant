# Curse of the Hollow King

**System:** D&D 5e
**Setting:** The Ashenmere Valley — a once-prosperous region now in decline. The capital, Greyhold, sits beneath a crumbling fortress. Strange disappearances started six months ago. Livestock found drained of blood. A cult operating in the ruins beneath the old fortress.

**Current arc:** The party has been hired by Mayor Hild of Greyhold to investigate the disappearances. They've traced the cult to the Bleakwood Forest and found evidence linking them to the Hollow King — a lich who was supposedly destroyed 200 years ago.

**Active plot threads:**
- The coded letter with the spider seal found in Klarg's chamber (not yet decoded)
- Sable's promise to return the seedling to Oldroot in the Bleakwood
- The missing shipment of silver from the Dwarven mines (connected to anti-undead weapons)
- Fendrel's connection to the Shadow Guild (party doesn't know yet)
- The Hollow King's phylactery — rumored to be hidden in the Tomb of Kael

**Key locations:**
- Greyhold: capital town, Mayor Hild's seat, market district, Charred Flagon tavern
- Bleakwood Forest: ancient treant Oldroot, cult activity, corrupted wildlife
- Tomb of Kael: sealed ancient tomb, rumored phylactery location
- Dwarven Mines of Khor-Dral: silver source, recent cave-ins, possible undead incursion

**House rules:**
- Critical hits: max damage die + roll
- Healing potions: bonus action to drink, action to administer
- Death saves: rolled privately by DM
- Flanking: +2 to hit (not advantage)

**Planned encounters this session:**
- Shadow Cultist patrol near the Bleakwood edge (4x Cultist, 1x Cult Fanatic)
- Possible Oldroot encounter if party enters deep Bleakwood
- Social: Mayor Hild confrontation about the missing silver shipment
