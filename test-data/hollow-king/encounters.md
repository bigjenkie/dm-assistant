# Planned Encounters

## Shadow Cultist Patrol (Combat)
**Location:** Bleakwood edge, along the old road
**Enemies:** 4x Cultist (AC 12, HP 9, scimitar +3 1d6+1), 1x Cult Fanatic (AC 13, HP 33, dagger +4 1d4+2, spells: command, inflict wounds, hold person)
**Difficulty:** Medium (adjusted for 4 level-6 PCs)
**Tactics:** Fanatic opens with hold person on the strongest melee. Cultists flank. If losing, fanatic tries to flee to warn the main camp.
**Loot:** Coded message (same cipher as the spider letter), 25gp, potion of healing

## Oldroot Encounter (Social/Exploration)
**Location:** Heartglade, deep Bleakwood
**Trigger:** Party enters the Heartglade or calls for Oldroot
**Mood:** Somber, ancient, the glade is visibly dying (brown leaves, cracked bark)
**Oldroot's ask:** Return the seedling. In exchange, a clue about the phylactery.
**If seedling returned:** Oldroot reveals "The tomb breathes where the river forgets its name" — pointing to a hidden entrance at a dry riverbed near the Tomb of Kael.
**If seedling not returned:** Oldroot is disappointed but patient. "The seasons wait for no one, but they wait longer than you think."

## Mayor Hild Confrontation (Social)
**Location:** Greyhold town hall, private meeting
**Trigger:** Party asks about the missing silver or confronts her about suspicious behavior
**DC 15 Insight:** She's hiding something. Nervous hands, won't make eye contact.
**DC 18 Persuasion:** She breaks down and confesses the blackmail. Begs the party to rescue her nephew.
**If party is hostile:** She doubles down on lies. Will attempt to have them arrested (she has 6 town guards).
