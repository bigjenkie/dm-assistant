# Oldroot
**Role:** Ancient guardian, prophecy source
**Race/Gender:** Treant, ageless (appears as an enormous gnarled oak)
**Personality:** Patient, cryptic, speaks in metaphors about seasons and growth
**Location:** Deep Bleakwood Forest, the Heartglade

Oldroot is the last of the ancient treants who once guarded the Ashenmere Valley. He spoke a prophecy about the Ashen Crown to the party: "The crown of cinders will be worn again before the leaves turn. The one who bears it must choose: dominion or dissolution." Sable promised to return a seedling to him — a sapling from the original World Tree.

**SECRET:** Oldroot knows the location of the Hollow King's phylactery but won't reveal it directly. He'll give clues if the seedling is returned. He's dying — the Hollow King's corruption is poisoning the Bleakwood, and Oldroot is the last thing holding it back.

**Quote:** "Even dead wood remembers the shape of what it was."
