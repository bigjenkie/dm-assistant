# Fendrel the Cartographer
**Role:** Information broker, secret Shadow Guild agent
**Race/Gender:** Half-elf male, late 30s
**Personality:** Charming, evasive, always has a deal to offer
**Location:** West market, Greyhold — "Fendrel's Maps & Curiosities"

Sold the party a map to the ruins for 50gp. The map was intentionally misleading — it led them into a cult ambush. Fendrel doesn't know the party survived. He works for the Shadow Guild, which has its own interest in the Hollow King's power.

**SECRET:** Fendrel is playing both sides. He works for the Shadow Guild but is also selling information to the cult. He'll betray whoever pays less. If cornered, he'll bargain with information about the Tomb of Kael's location.

**Quote:** "Every map tells a story. Some of them are even true."
