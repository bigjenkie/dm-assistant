# Mayor Hild
**Role:** Quest giver, political authority in Greyhold
**Race/Gender:** Human female, mid-50s
**Personality:** Stern, pragmatic, hides her fear behind competence
**Location:** Greyhold town hall

She hired the party to investigate the disappearances. Offered 500gp for results. She's more worried than she lets on — her own nephew was among the disappeared. She suspects the cult but won't say it publicly because Greyhold's economy depends on travelers feeling safe.

**SECRET:** Mayor Hild is being blackmailed by the Hollow King's agents. They have her nephew. She's been feeding them information about the party's movements in exchange for proof of life. She's desperate and will eventually crack and confess if the party earns her trust.

**Quote:** "I don't need theories, I need answers. And I need them before the harvest festival."
